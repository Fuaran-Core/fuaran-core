# Checkpoint replay cost — 2026-11-14 — i7-8650U — Phase 662 (an indexed replay from a checkpoint reads the region above it)

Phase 662 asked whether `Dag.replayFromWith`, a replay from a checkpoint over the reachability index, costs
the history below the checkpoint rather than the region above it, and, if the checkpoint's ancestor set was
the dominant cost, to read the region from the index's bitsets instead. The figure it was filed on came from a
consumer on Core `0.42.0` (one replay allocating 14.5 MB at 20,000 records, 13.7 MB of it the ancestor set);
the "before" table below reproduces it in this repository.

- **Machine:** Intel Core i7-8650U @ 1.90 GHz, 8 logical processors, Windows 10.0.26300.
- **.NET:** SDK 10.0.203, Release build of `tests/Fuaran.Core.Tests`.
- **Trees:** before, fuaran-core `phase-662` at `f9838d9` with only the measurement leg added; after, the same
  with this phase's `src/` change.
- **Shape:** `shared` records of linear history (genesis included) under SHA-256, a checkpoint at its tip —
  the fork — two lanes of 50 appends each off the fork, and their merge as the head; indexed once with
  `Reach.ofDag`. The region above the checkpoint is 101 nodes at every size.
- **Command:** one process per size,
  `FUARAN_CORE_MEASURE_REPLAY_FROM=<shared> dotnet tests/Fuaran.Core.Tests/bin/Release/net10.0/Fuaran.Core.Tests.dll --filter-test-case "checkpoint replay measurement"`.
  Each call is warmed three times; the bytes are one call's `GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread` delta and
  the time the mean of 50 calls. The leg checks first that the replay equals the full replay's state.
- **Columns:** shared history; region above the checkpoint; one `replayFromWith`'s bytes and microseconds;
  beside it, one `Reach.ancestors` at the checkpoint — the set the replay built before this phase.

## Before

| shared | region | replay bytes | replay us | ancestor-set bytes | ancestor-set us |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1000 | 101 | 537512 | 5598.1 | 463728 | 2984.9 |
| 5000 | 101 | 3144256 | 30877.9 | 2940440 | 21637.0 |
| 20000 | 101 | 14391824 | 99089.4 | 13709368 | 74510.4 |

The premise holds: at 20,000 records the ancestor set is 95 per cent of the replay's allocation (13.7 of
14.4 MB, the consumer's figure) and three quarters of its time, and the replay grows 26.8 times in
allocation from 1,000 to 20,000 records for a fixed region.

## After

| shared | region | replay bytes | replay us | ancestor-set bytes | ancestor-set us |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1000 | 101 | 5848 | 132.0 | 463728 | 2300.7 |
| 5000 | 101 | 5848 | 149.2 | 2940440 | 16104.3 |
| 20000 | 101 | 5848 | 344.5 | 13709368 | 65908.4 |

The replay's allocation no longer moves with the history: 5,848 bytes at every size, a 20,000 / 1,000 ratio of
1.0 (it was 26.8), and 2,461 times less than before at 20,000 records. Its time grows 2.6 times over a 20-fold
history: what remains linear is the word-by-word comparison of the head's and the checkpoint's bitsets (one
32-bit word per 32 nodes, compared in place) and the logarithmic id lookups of the fold. The ancestor-set
column is unchanged, as it should be: `Reach.ancestors` itself is not touched, the replay simply no longer
calls it.

The allocation pin in `ReachTests.fs` holds the shape in the ordinary suite: at 500 and 4,000 records of
shared history the replay's allocation may differ by less than a quarter, and must be under a tenth of the
checkpoint's closure.
