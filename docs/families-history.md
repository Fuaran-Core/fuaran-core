# How the law-family roster came to read as it does

`Fuaran.Core.Families` declares each law family once: what it takes, who elects it, how its sample can
starve, where its refusals come from, which ladder rows it discharges and which operations it is
evidence for. The record says what is true now. This page says why, one line per phase that moved a
row or the roster's shape, oldest first. Until Phase 506 these notes sat beside the rows in
`Families.fs`; the record and its generated renderings (`conformance-families.md`, `.json`) are the
current statement, and this page is the archaeology behind it.

- **Phase 161** — `containerLaws`: every arm is built, but whether the witness honours the rebuild is drawn (a `ReplaceChildren` partial on leaves reaches none), so the family counts what each arm reached and guards it rather than claiming `Unconditional`.
- **Phase 184** — the roster is created and held to reflection over RETURN TYPE; `opAlgebra`, `reducer` and `compositionPilot` join it, absent until then because the old completeness check read method names ending in `Laws`.
- **Phase 189** — `keyedChildrenLawsAt`: the collision arms are built through the domain's `PlaceKeyedChild`; a witness declaring no keyed position reports that as its adequacy line, a declaration rather than a missed verdict.
- **Phase 194** — `reason` is added and `OptIn` derived from it, so an opt-in family cannot be declared without saying why; `NeedsSecondFixture` is deliberately not a case, since no family needs one.
- **Phase 196** — `cases` joins the export; `attestationLawsAt` moves to `Guarded`, because the sink is a parameter and `OpStream.noAttestation` leaves four of its five laws asserting nothing.
- **Phase 209** — `propagationEvalLaws`' second guarded dimension is the undeclared-read refusal: a real node read without being declared, and an id the map does not hold.
- **Phase 211** — `propagationEvaluatorLawsAt`: every arm the agreement law distinguishes is drawn from the domain's own edits, so the family is guarded.
- **Phase 220** — the refusable-family audit; the two base-run families leave `Unconditional`, because every law reading the apply outcome is quantified over a population the domain's generator draws.
- **Phase 223** — the six drawn-refusal families the audit found each count their accepted and refused cases and guard them, with each kit reference generator stratified so the guard never fires on it.
- **Phase 232** — `witnessSurfaceLaws` is rostered as `NoWitnessToCertify`: it certifies the Core a domain compiled against, and nothing is drawn.
- **Phase 245** — `streamLaws` and `laneFoldLawsWith` are guarded: a generator refused every time leaves every chain empty and the laws green over nothing.
- **Phase 246** — the seam families at a domain's seam are rostered beside their fixture forms; `aiSurfaceLawsAt` runs the domain's own `Decide`, and the kit-policy form keeps the Phase 223 dimensions.
- **Phase 247** — `keyedArbitrationLawsAt`: the container-illegal proposal and the keyed-id clash are built, the rest of the rejected bucket drawn.
- **Phase 249** — `footprintLawsAt` certifies a domain's hand-written footprint, guarded on the independent pair and the interfering pair.
- **Phase 250** — the prior-aware evaluator family runs the evaluator family first, so its reference arms carry that family's guard too.
- **Phase 257** — the kit splits across two packages: `Roster` is a package's share, the export's `package` becomes `packages` and each family names its own (`schema: 5`); `aggregateNullSkipLaws` is the `Column.aggregate` half of what was `aggregateParityLaws`.
- **Phase 276** — `aggregateNullSkipLaws` is guarded per present cell type, since the column type is drawn and the decimal `Sum` shares no fold.
- **Phase 286** — `keyedApplyLawsAt`: the collisions the unkeyed engine is blind to are built through `PlaceKeyedChild`; `witness-surface-scope` moves to the declaration-agreement family.
- **Phase 288** — the lane-DAG checkpoint family: a covered replay over a merge, an uncovered refusal and a compaction are demanded by the DAG-shape guard.
- **Phase 289** — the reachability-index family: a merge of incomparable lanes is counted and guarded.
- **Phase 297** — each row declares its adequacy class and refusal verdict, which become projections (`SampleAdequacy.census`, `refusalAudit`); `hashFnLaws`, `attributedLaws`, `encoderInjectivityLawsAt` and `captureReplayLaws` leave `Unconditional` (their reorder, re-attribution, distinct-tree and tamper arms are the generator's to reach); `snapshotLawsWith` and `dagLaws` read through strict runner cells; `casLaws` counts its race arm; `reconcileLawsWith` takes the domain's chain hash; `WireNullTolerance.laws` is rostered.
- **Phase 298** — the observer seam's family: every arm is built each iteration.
- **Phase 302** — `diffLaws` and `normalizeLaws` are guarded (every law holds over an identity or an all-refused script); `independence-diamond`'s discharge moves from `footprintLaws` to `footprintLawsAt`; `hashFnLaws` carries `content-id-determines-content` and discharges nothing.
- **Phase 311** — the lane store: the lane-shape guard demands a cross-lane merge, two heads, a fork handed to one lane and a content collision.
- **Phase 312** — the placement algebra, tree lowering and fresh ids are `StrongerPromise`: each takes the base witness and promises more.
- **Phase 313** — the structural-integrity strand: the grammar family takes the base witness, the reference family the `RefWitness`.
- **Phase 314** — the digest maps and change classification take the base witness and the domain's content encoder; the rule-registry gate is a seam; each is guarded on the shape of the pairs it drew.
- **Phase 318** — the policy gate, the no-unapproved-write law, the write gate and the keyed capture journal are rostered.
- **Phase 335** — the operation roster: each family lists the public operations its laws are about, the law-family leg of the proof leg's operation coverage.
- **Phase 349** — the sanitisation floor at a host's `SanitizeWitness`, the stream `…With` operations at the caller's config and hash (`streamConfigLaws`), and the string-escape vectors rostered as laws.
- **Phase 360** — the encoding-profile vectors and the stored-identity families, whose sample is the store walked whole.
- **Phase 379** — the stored-codec family, run against a consumer's own corpus.
- **Phase 390** — the naming rule: each witness-taking row is spelled `…At` (the bare forwards left at `0.37.0`, Phase 386); each vector family's `…With` carries a corpus law so an empty hand-in is red; the cross-pipeline value table is rostered.
- **Phase 417** — the typed column: three pairs are built every iteration, so no run can miss the NaN or the signed zero.
- **Phase 418** — the ownership contract: one assertion per column over every element.
- **Phase 442** — `codecLaws` at a domain's `Codec<'T>`: opt-in as a seam (its codec and generator are not witness types), guarded on the mutation outcome.
- **Phase 454** — `streamLaws`' JSONL cell discharges the op-stream load model's premises at a domain's own witness.
- **Phase 498** — the export's outside reader is recorded, and a `schema` bump becomes a recorded contract move.
- **Phase 500** — the base-aware footprint family, guarded on the pair only the base-aware footprint frees.
- **Phase 506** — one record per family: `Takes` and `Election` replace the witness names and the opt-in pair, the operation roster folds into `Operations`, `Spellings` is added, and the export is written by `Canon.render` (`schema: 6`); this page takes the row commentary.
- **Phase 507** — six pinned-default twins (`reconcileLaws`, `concurrencyLaws`, `snapshotLaws`, `keyedArbitrationLawsAt`, `columnOwnershipLaws`, `laneFoldLawsAt`) become `Spellings` of their `…With` row; `propagationEvaluatorLawsWith` becomes `propagationPriorLawsAt` and `codecLaws` (now taking `Takes.Codec`, so `NeedsWitnessCapability`) becomes `codecLawsAt`, each old name an obsolete forward on its own row.
