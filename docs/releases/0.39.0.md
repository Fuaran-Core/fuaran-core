## 0.39.0 — DRAFT

The minor after the released `0.38.1`. It opens over `v0.38.1` and carries the slices of the 2026-10-17
redesign pass. Through the `0.x` line a breaking class rides a minor ([`STABILITY.md`](../../STABILITY.md),
"Versioning policy"), and this slot opens on one: a renderer that now refuses a value it used to write.

### Breaking

- **The canonical writer and the IDL encoder refuse a repeated member name** (Phase 495, `Fuaran.Core.Wire`,
  `Fuaran.Core.ContentAddress`, `Fuaran.Core.Idl`) — the third refused class, which `Json.tryRender` and
  `Canon.tryRender` have refused since Phase 462 (`0.38.1`, under Breaking on the same footing).
  `Canonical.tryWrite`, and so `ContentAddress.ofValue`, now refuse a value carrying one member name
  twice; before, they digested text that `Json.parse` refuses, so an address could name bytes no reader
  accepts. `Idl.Encode.encode` and `encodeOp` refuse a map value, or a verbatim json value, that carries
  one key twice, naming the key and its path; before, they wrote wire that `Decode.decode` refuses. No
  public signature changes and no wire byte moves for any value that was readable before.
  **Migration:** a caller that built such a value was already producing text no reader accepts; build the
  value with one member per name, or handle the refusal the call now returns.
- **The query seam's two policy refusals carry their seam on the wire** (Phase 497, `Fuaran.Core.Query`).
  `QueryError.QueryPolicyRefused` and `QueryApprovalRequired` encoded to `$type` `policyRefused` and
  `approvalRequired`, the tags of the capability seam's `InvokeError.PolicyRefused` / `ApprovalRequired`,
  with the same members, so a refusal document did not say which seam refused and each seam's reader
  accepted the other's (measured: all four cross-readings decoded). The query seam's tags are now
  `queryPolicyRefused` and `queryApprovalRequired` (the case-name prefix D101 gave the cases, carried onto
  the wire); the capability seam's are unchanged; `QueryError.describe` is unchanged; and each reader
  refuses the other seam's tags as an unknown `$type`. The `api/wire/Fuaran.Core.Query.txt` rows move.
  A policy refusal is raised before the resolver runs and is never captured, so no keyed-capture journal
  holds one. **Migration:** a consumer that reads query-seam refusal documents matches the new tags; one
  that wrote them writes the new tags.
- **`ColumnValidator.ofType` reports the column, not its cells** (Phase 497, `Fuaran.Core.Validator`) — a
  verdict change. The rule read each present cell's type back from its text, and a millisecond (or finer)
  timestamp column renders a whole-second instant with no fraction, which reads back as seconds: every
  such row of a column OF the declared type was a `COL-OFTYPE` error. Since Phase 417 a present cell is
  always of its column's type, so the rule now answers the one fact it can: a column whose type is not
  the declared one is ONE `COL-OFTYPE` error located at the column (its name, not `column#row`); a column
  of the declared type reports nothing, and a column of another type is reported even with no present
  row (the cell walk had nothing to read there). Measured before the fix: a millisecond column with two
  whole-second rows reported two errors. The code, the severity, the message's form (`got` now names
  the column's type) and the rule id are unchanged. **Migration:** a caller counting `COL-OFTYPE` findings per row, or reading their `#row`
  location, reads one finding at the column.
- **The family roster is one record, its export is canonical, and the witness freeze has one literal**
  (Phase 506, `Fuaran.Core.Conformance`) — removal and record reshaping, plus a wire move of the export.
  `Families.LawFamily` is declared as `Id`, `Takes` (a closed `Families.Takes` union of the witness,
  generator and sink types a family takes), `Election` (`BaseRun` | `OptIn of OptInReason`), `Adequacy`,
  `Refusal`, `Discharges`, `Operations` and `Spellings` (empty on every row today); `Module`, `Entry`,
  `Witness`, `OptIn`, `Reason` and `RefusalAudit` are members read off it, so every reader of a family
  compiles unchanged and only a construction moves. `Families.operations` and `FamilyOperations` are
  removed (a family's operations are its record's `Operations`), as are `refusalAuditOf` and `censusOf`;
  `Roster` keeps `Package` and `Families`, with `RefusalAudit` and `Census` now members.
  `docs/conformance-families.json` is `schema: 6`: every value written by `Canon.render`, members in
  ordinal order, one family per line, and a `spellings` array on each family; the ids, `cases` and every
  other member's value are unchanged, and the one outside reader (it parses by name) reads the same
  roster. `Conformance.declaredWitnessFields` is removed: the Fable pipeline's freeze law reads
  `frozenWitnessFields` itself, and this repository's suite holds the pins to reflection and to
  `frozen-witness-fields.txt`. **Migration:** a package that declares its own roster share builds each
  row as `{ Id = "<Module>.<entry>"; Takes = [ Families.Takes.StreamGen ]; Election = Families.OptIn
  Families.SeamNotEveryDomainHas; Adequacy = ...; Refusal = ...; Discharges = []; Operations = [];
  Spellings = [] }` (drop `Module`, `Entry`, `Witness`, `OptIn`, `Reason`) and its `Roster` as
  `{ Package = ...; Families = ... }`; a reader of the export reads members by name, not position; a
  reader of `declaredWitnessFields` reads `frozenWitnessFields`.
- **The law runner carries the family id, and `…With` has one meaning** (Phase 507,
  `Fuaran.Core.Conformance`) — `union-widening`, plus additions and a wire move of the export.
  `Families.Takes` gains `Codec` (declared last): an exhaustive `match` over it gains a case.
  `Conformance.codecLaws` and `EncodingProfileVectors.storedCodecLaws` take the domain's `Codec<'T>`
  as a witness capability, so they are spelled `codecLawsAt` and `storedCodecLawsAt` (D127, D148.3
  amended); `Conformance.propagationEvaluatorLawsWith` ran
  `propagationEvaluatorLawsAt` and added laws, a different law set, so it is
  `Conformance.propagationPriorLawsAt`. The old names are `[<Obsolete>]` forwards, each with its own
  roster row, census cell and guard label, so nothing that pins them moves. Six pinned-default twins
  become `Spellings` of their `…With` family: `reconcileLaws`, `concurrencyLaws`, `snapshotLaws`,
  `keyedArbitrationLawsAt`, `columnOwnershipLaws` and `FoldConfluence.laneFoldLawsAt` stay public entry
  points with unchanged signatures and are read as one family, one census cell and one reference run

  labelled with the family id, so `keyedArbitrationLawsAt`'s guard now reads
  `Conformance.keyedArbitrationLawsWith`, as the other five already did. Inside the kit every guard is
  minted by the runner from the id the public entry point hands it, and every derived seed is
  `Runner.Fork k` at the seed the inline offset used to compute: every other family's results, every
  counterexample stamp and every committed `conformance/` vector are byte-identical.
  **Migration:** a consumer census that enumerates the kit by reflection gains three families at this
  raise, `Conformance.codecLawsAt`, `Conformance.propagationPriorLawsAt` and
  `EncodingProfileVectors.storedCodecLawsAt`, and adds a row for each; `codecLaws`,
  `propagationEvaluatorLawsWith` and `storedCodecLaws` remain as `[<Obsolete>]` forwards, each with its
  own roster row, until the `1.0` retirement. A reader of the export that keyed on one of the six spellings'
  ids reads it from its family's `spellings`.
- **A union, record or enum is annotatable as a TYPE** (Phase 542, `Fuaran.Core.Idl`,
  `Fuaran.Core.Idl.Codegen`) — record widening; additive on the wire and in the artifact. `IdlUnion`,
  `IdlRecord` and `IdlEnum` gain `Annotations: Annotations` (the set members carry; an enum's sits beside
  its `CaseAnnotations`), so a FULL record literal of any of the three no longer compiles (FS0764);
  `Declare.enumOf` / `enumWith` and every `{ x with … }` construction compile unchanged. `idl.json` writes
  the set as an optional `annotations` member on the union, record or enum object, omitted when empty, so
  every existing artifact is byte-identical and reads back unchanged. The F# type emitter prints the set
  above the type (the support doc first, then the doc and the notes as `///` lines) and a deprecated or
  in-process-only type earns the `System.Obsolete` its members earn, with the module's `#nowarn "44"`;
  the TypeScript declarations print it as a JSDoc block above the `export type`. An unannotated
  vocabulary's emission is byte-identical. **Migration:** a vocabulary that builds one of the three by
  record literal adds `Annotations = Annotations.Empty`; one that documents a type moves the prose into
  the type's `Annotations.Doc`.
- **The stability diff grades a type's own annotations** (Phase 548, `Fuaran.Core.Idl.Codegen`) — record
  widening and a union case added; nothing on the wire or in the artifact moves. `Diff` read the
  annotations of fields, union cases, kinds, ops and enum cases but not the type-level set Phase 542 added,
  so a whole union, record or enum becoming deprecated (or in-process-only, or re-documented) between two
  artifacts was reported as `unchanged`. `Diff.Snapshot` gains `TypeAnnotations` (keyed `(family, name)`,

  `…Removed`. `docs/idl-stability-classes.md` gains the row. **Migration:** a full record literal of
  `Diff.Snapshot` adds `TypeAnnotations = Map.empty` (FS0764 otherwise); an exhaustive `match` over
  `Diff.Change` adds the case.
- **The F* proof model is the decoder the hosts implement** (Phase 525, `Fuaran.Core.Idl.Codegen`; reads
  D174's `Decl`). `FStarTarget` no longer walks the vocabulary itself: its model is printed from the
  wire reach of `Decl.resolve`, in the first-reach order it always used. A hosted slot that DECLARES its
  wire form is modelled at that form, so the model's decoder checks the type the F# and TypeScript
  decoders check before the codec runs, where it used to carry any JSON value; a declared wire form no host
  can read is refused with the hosts' own refusal, and a declared string format is refused by name (the
  model's string has no format predicate). A declared default is admitted by `Decl.literal`, the one
  admissibility the F# backend now reads too: the model refuses what that backend refuses, which is an
  enum default spelled by its host case name where the wire spells it differently, an empty map, a
  closure or opaque value, and the union's declared transparent case. Before, it emitted a model for all
  four. No public signature moves. The certification set reaches none of these, so the committed
  `proofs/*.fst` are byte-identical and the proof leg did not move. The UI vocabulary's
  `Binding<…>`-wired `FormFieldKind` values are such slots: a model over the kinds that reach them now has
  `Binding` at their declared argument, and the `FStarTarget.proofKinds` header names `u_binding__l_str`
  among the types `Filters` and `Form` would add. **Migration:** regenerate a committed model.
  A kind refused by the new rules is named in the model's header, like any other refused kind.

### Additive

- **A base-aware footprint, and the arbitration and fold entries that hold the base** (Phase 500,
  `Fuaran.Core.Ops`, `Fuaran.Core.OpStream.Dag`, `Fuaran.Core.Conformance`; D166). `Ops.footprintIn` /
  `footprintInKeyed` compute a script's footprint at the tree it applies to, threaded op by op: a removal
  or a move names its source parent as a known structure-write, a removal content-writes the subtree it
  destroys, a move reads its destination's ancestors, an in-place `UpdateNode` reads and content-writes
  its target alone, and an absent target — or any op after one that does not apply — takes the pure
  arm, so the result is never less conservative than `Ops.footprint` where the tree is silent. Two
  in-place edits of different nodes under different parents are independent; an edit beside the
  removal of its ancestor is not. `Arbitration.arbitrateIn`, `Dag.conflictsIn`, `Dag.reconcileIn` and
  `Dag.reconcileManyIn` compose it (the last two replay the shared region before reading a footprint,
  since that is the state the exclusive deltas apply to); `Conformance.footprintInLawsAt` certifies
  soundness, refinement of the pure projection and determinism at a domain's own ops. Proved:
  `FootprintIn.footprint_in_refines` (no pair the pure footprint frees is refused) and
  `footprint_in_sound` over every operation, moves included (the five move pairs were proved in the
  phase's continuation, on the facts that each edit distributes over a graft elsewhere and that a move
  keeps every other node, parent and path in place). The pure entries and every verdict they give are
  unchanged; no public signature changes.

  public signature changes.
