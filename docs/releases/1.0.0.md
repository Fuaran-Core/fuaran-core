## 1.0.0 — DRAFT

The first major. It opens over the released `0.36.0` and is the one slot where a removal is free:
the `0.x` line's scheduled removals land here, and from this slot the version is semver
([`STABILITY.md`](../../STABILITY.md), "Versioning policy"). A consumer's edits, from any `0.x`
pin back to `0.20.0`, are [the 1.0.0 migration](../migrations/1.0.0.md).

### The removal sweep, gated by the `OneDotZero` family (Phase 386) — `removal` on seven packages, `retype`-free; source-breaking qualification on six unions; the wire `removal` of one retired document, every other byte unchanged

**The class, from the gate.** The surface family prints, against `v0.36.0`: `Fuaran.Core.Conformance`
**`removal`** (24 moves), `Fuaran.Core.Function` **`removal`** (10), `Fuaran.Core.Observer`
**`removal`** (17), `Fuaran.Core.OpStream` **`removal`** (19), `Fuaran.Core.OpStream.Dag` **`removal`**
(9, with `Dag.ofNodes` and `Dag.KeyMismatch` additive), `Fuaran.Core.Query` **`removal`** (3) and
`Fuaran.Core.Wire` **`removal`** (13). The major pays for every one. The wire-surface baseline of
`Fuaran.Core.Wire` drops the `rows` document, which only the removed `RowCodec` emitted; no other
wire baseline, digest, chain pre-image, conformance vector or `ParityVectors` row moves (the law
corpora re-stamp `kitVersion` and nothing else).

**What left.** Every `[<Obsolete>]` member of every shipped assembly — 44, found by reflection, not
by a list:

- the 19 pre-Phase-296 snapshot entry points on `OpStream` (`snapshotAt`, `compact`, `verifyAcross`,
  `snapshotToJsonl`, `snapshotFromJsonl`, `replayFrom` and their `…ChainOnly` / `…With` / `…Opt`
  variants) — `OpStream.Snapshots` takes the mode and the config as arguments;
- `Dag.replayTo`, which raised on an unknown head or a cyclic history — `Dag.tryReplayTo`;
- `RowCodec`, whose bytes depended on the machine's time zone and widened an `int64` past 2^53 to a
  double, with the `Row` alias that only it used;
- the `Registry` module, `CapabilityRegistry`'s name until Phase 295;
- the 22 bare and `…With` law-family forwards (Phases 297 and 390) — each `…At` spelling, under its
  own roster id; their roster rows go with them.

Beside them the one-draft items that carried no attribute: the string-error `Decode` helpers
(`getProp`, `asString`, `asInt`, `asBool`, `asFloat`, `kindOf`, `strField`, `intField`, `mapList`) —
`Decoder`, described with `Decoder.describing` where a string error is wanted; the
`Validator.Registry` alias — `Validator.RuleRegistry`; and the `Fuaran.Core.Observer` namespace's
`IObserver` / `InMemoryObserver` adapter — the `ObserverWitness` functions, with subscription kept
by the host. The observer family's two adapter laws went with the adapter: the family now holds
every emission and snapshot to the derivation of its input, and an unregistered node to none.

**What closed.** `CapabilityRegistry` and `QueryRegistry` are opaque, with `FunctionRegistry`'s
shape: `register` — and its admission gate — is the only way in. `Dag.T`'s constructor is private;
`Dag.ofNodes` builds one from a node map and refuses a key that is not its node's id (a node whose id
does not match its content is admitted — that is `firstBreak`'s question). `Nodes` is still read
freely. `witnessFieldsLaw` and `witnessCoverageLaw` left the `Conformance` facade, which no longer
publishes a `System.Type`; `witnessSurfaceLaws ()` runs both.

**What was qualified (source-breaking, invisible to the surface gate).** `[<RequireQualifiedAccess>]`
now marks `PipelineError`, `PipelineEvalError`, `SchemaCompat`, `Versioning.Evolution`,
`WireNullTolerance.Claim` and `Idl.Diff.Strength` — every union whose case name a second public union
also carried unqualified. A new family in `Public surface` holds the rule: no case name is carried
unqualified by two public unions of the shipped assemblies. The surface renderer does not draw the
attribute, so these moves read as `unchanged` above; they are named here instead.

**What left one phase later.** The lenient read of a value space's descriptor spelling (`"kind"`,
`minLength` / `maxLength`) was scheduled with these and kept at first, because the verified model of
that reader pinned it (DECISIONS.md D133). It left on this slot too, with the model's arm, its
vector and the oracle; its own block records it (Phase 405, DECISIONS.md D134).

**`OneDotZero`.** A new test family reads the major of `<Version>` and, from major 1, holds four
laws: no obsolete member; every witness frozen; the frozen field lists byte-equal to the major's
`vN.0.0` tag (through `tests/Fuaran.Core.Tests/frozen-witness-fields.txt`, held to the live list at
every major); and no breaking baseline move since the newest tag without a major advance. Each law
was watched red before the sweep (44 obsolete members at `<Version>1.0.0`) and has a go-red plant.

### A kind projection carries its message map and declares its record's fields (Phase 403) — BREAKING-SOURCE: `Gen.KindProjection` gains `MapMsg` and `RecordFields` (`record-widening`); the support document gains two optional keys (`additive`); an emission over a projected kind that cannot read its record now refuses

**The class, from the gate.** The surface family prints `Fuaran.Core.Idl.Codegen` **`record-widening`**:
`Gen.KindProjection` gains the record fields `MapMsg: string option` and
`RecordFields: IdlField list option`, so its constructor is a `retype` and every full-literal
construction of a projection stops compiling (`FS0764`) until it names both. The wire family prints
the `supportArtifact` baseline **`additive`**: a projection may now carry `mapMsg` and
`recordFields`, and a projection declaring neither renders byte for byte as it did, so every
existing `support.json` reads unchanged under encoding version 1. No other baseline, wire byte or
committed generated module moves.

**Why.** A kind projection (`Gen.KindProjection`) replaces a kind's record, encoder and decoder with
host source. The generator then has no way to know the record's shape, yet three emissions read it:

- `Derivation.MapMsg` refused a vocabulary with a projected kind ("the message map over the
  projected kind …"), and a projection had no member through which a host could supply the one
  piece the generator cannot construct. A host with a projected kind kept a hand-written message
  map beside its otherwise generated layer — the walk a new kind can be missed by.
- `Derivation.SlotsOf`, the node witness (`children` / `withChildren`, private or under
  `StructuralAccess`) and `KeyedPositions` read the kind's WIRE fields as if they were the
  record's. Where the two differ — a wire key optional where the record's member is required, two
  wire keys merged into one member — the emission did not compile: a host's `SlotsOf "Binding"`
  produced `match s.On with Some …` against a required `On` (`FS0001`). A generator that emits
  source that does not compile is a defect, not a limitation.

The design, the refusal rules and the rejected alternatives are DECISIONS.md D132.

**What moved, for a consumer.**

- **`KindProjection.MapMsg`** — the full `and private mapMsg<Tag>Spec<'Msg, 'Msg2> (f: 'Msg -> 'Msg2)
  (v: <Tag>Spec<'Msg>) : <Tag>Spec<'Msg2> = …` member, verbatim, written once in host source beside
  the record as `Mk` already is. `Derivation.MapMsg` joins it to the derived message map's
  recursion group, so it may call `mapMsg f` and every derived `mapMsg<Decl> f`. With `None`, the
  refusal stands with the same construct, principle and alternative: the generator does not guess a
  map.
- **`KindProjection.RecordFields`** — the projected record's fields at their HOST shapes (name as a
  wire field is spelled, the member being its PascalCase; type; optionality). The witness, the keyed
  walk and the slot enumerators read these in place of the kind's wire fields. A declared field at a
  type the module does not declare is refused by name ("the projected record field 'Gate.owner' at
  the undeclared type 'Owner'").
- **With `RecordFields = None`, an emission that would read the record refuses by name** instead of
  emitting source that may not compile: the node witness and `StructuralAccess` when the kind's wire
  holds a node directly ("the node witness's children of the projected kind …"), `KeyedPositions`
  when it holds one anywhere ("the keyed node positions of the projected kind …"), and `SlotsOf`
  always ("a slot enumerator over 'T' through the projected kind …"), because a record may hold a
  value of the type that its wire spells otherwise. A projected kind whose wire holds no node still
  emits a plain module with no declaration: a node on no wire key is a node no host reads back, so
  the record holds none.
- **`support.json`** carries the two members as `mapMsg` and `recordFields` on a projection entry,
  the fields in `idl.json`'s own field shape (one codec for both documents), and `Diff` compares
  both as part of the projection's support entry.

**Adopting it.** Name both members in every full-literal `KindProjection` (`MapMsg = None;
RecordFields = None` keeps today's emission wherever no refusal above applies). A host whose
projected kind's wire holds a node — `Switch` in the UI vocabulary, whose `default` is one —
declares that kind's `RecordFields` to keep its witness and `StructuralAccess`, and supplies
`MapMsg` to request `Derivation.MapMsg` and retire its hand-written map.

**Evidence.** `IdlDeriveTests`, "Phase 403 - a projected kind under the derivations": a vocabulary
projecting `Gate`, whose wire `rule` is optional where the record's `Rule` is required, emitted
with the map and the record declared and RUN under `dotnet fsi` (the walks, the rule slot, the
mapped handler and the encoding, line for line); the same probe over the pre-403 reading (the wire
fields taken as the record's) fails to compile with `FS0001`, so the probe tells the two apart; the
refusal with no map, with its unchanged message; each refusal with no declared record, by name;
the undeclared-type refusal; the support document's round trip; and the diff seeing either member.

### A value space has one reader spelling (Phase 405) — wire `removal` on the `Function` reader: `SpaceCodec.decoder` refuses the descriptor spelling; no public member, writer, digest or canonical document moves

**The class.** The surface family prints `Fuaran.Core.Function` **`unchanged`**: the reader's
public signature is the same, and the only members that moved are internal or private
(`SpaceCodec.decoderSaying` and the case table). The move is a `removal` on the WIRE READER, so
it is named here rather than read off a baseline. A document `SpaceCodec.toJson` writes reads back
byte for byte as before.

**What left.** `SpaceCodec.decoder` read a value space in two spellings. One was the document
`toJson` writes (`"$type"`, `min` / `max`). The other was the descriptor spelling `toSchema` writes
(`"kind"`, a string length as `minLength` / `maxLength`), read leniently when `"$type"` was absent.
It now reads the first only. A document with a `"kind"` and no `"$type"` is refused with the
codec's existing fault, `MissingField` at `$type`, wherever a value space is read: a capability's
hole, the typed refusals `EmptySpace` and `ArgOutOfSpace`, and a pipeline node's `outputType`.
Freezing two reader spellings of one wire type for the 1.x line is what every other move on this
slot refused.

**What stayed.** `SpaceCodec.descriptorJson`, and `toSchema`'s bytes through it, because
`ContentPack.signatureFingerprint` hashes them (D104). It is a descriptor, written and never read.

**The model.** `space_of_j` in `proofs/Capability.fst` lost its descriptor arm and is the `"$type"`
dispatch alone. The lemmas over it (`space_roundtrip` and the decoded-value lemmas that read
through it) are unchanged in statement and re-verified, three cold runs, every query 3/3 under
`--quake`. The oracle is re-extracted, and the differential host holds it to production. The
pinning vector `the-descriptor-spelling-of-a-space-is-read-leniently` is now
`the-descriptor-spelling-of-a-space-is-refused-at-type`.

**Evidence.** `FunctionTests`, "the capability codec refuses an unknown hole kind; the space reader
refuses the descriptor spelling": the descriptor spelling of every space, each case and both
`SlotTree` forms, is refused `MissingField` at `$type`. The document spelling reads back in the
same case. The model's twin states the same refusal and the oracle host evaluates it. A consumer's
edit is in [the 1.0.0 migration](../migrations/1.0.0.md).
