## 0.40.3 — DRAFT

**Slot class: additive.** The patch after the released `0.40.2`. It opens over `v0.40.2`, which is a
consumer's contract, so nothing rides it; the work in this slot is additive, which makes it a PATCH slot, and
a member of a higher class advances it to the `0.41.0` minor rather than riding it ([`STABILITY.md`](../../STABILITY.md),
"Versioning policy"). Phases 487, 489 and 491 open it. The conformance `kitVersion` stamp lines move to `0.40.3`
with the slot, and the corpus pin moves to the re-stamp of the declared copies.

### Additive

- **A hole's signature entry is public: `SigEntry.ofHoleDecl`** (Phase 487, `Fuaran.Core.Function`; DECISIONS.md
  D214) — class additive; the wire none. `Function.signature` read each declared hole into its `SigEntry` inside a
  private local, so a consumer needing one hole's entry wrote the mapping again and could drift from it.
  `SigEntry.ofHoleDecl : HoleDecl -> SigEntry` is that mapping, made public, and `Function.signature` maps it over
  the witness's holes. Its rules: address and label as declared; the kind as declared (`Space` follows from it);
  value and slot holes required; a repeat required exactly when its count space is a count space (`Space.isCount`);
  an action hole not required on the data axis. No signature, schema, codec or corpus byte moves.
- **A finding carries the recovery its rule stated** (Phase 489, `Fuaran.Core.Validator`; DECISIONS.md D215,
  applying D184.8) — class additive; the wire none. `Validator.Finding<'Id> = { Defect; Guidance:
  RejectionGuidance option }` with `Finding.ofDefect` / `Finding.guided`; `Validator.GuidedCheck<'Subject,'Id>`
  (a rule answering findings) and `Validator.asGuided`; on `Validator.Rules`: `registerGuided`, `replaceGuided`,
  `ofGuided`, `tryFindGuided`, `runFindings`. `Rules.run` is `runFindings` read back as defects and `Rules.tryFind`
  answers a guided rule's defects, so every defect code, order and `canonicalCodes` string is unchanged; a thrown
  rule's `RULE-FAULT` is unguided. `Defect` is not widened. `Conformance.introducedLaws` gains the guided-walk cell
  (`docs/conformance-families.json`: its `cases` 1000 → 1200).
- **What a signed claim is about: the attestation descriptor** (Phase 491, `Fuaran.Core.OpStream`; DECISIONS.md
  D216, applying D187.7) — class additive. `AttestationClaim` names the stream, the segment's head (an `Anchor`:
  one past its last record, and that record's hash), the party, the algorithm id, the signed-at instant (unix
  seconds), the key id and the evidence verdicts; sealed at the anchor of the segment it covers it is an
  `AttestationDescriptor` (`Sealed<AttestationClaim>`, always `Strict`), by the sealed state's one sealing step
  over the pre-image `{"attestation":1,"seq":...,"claim":...}` (`AttestationDescriptor.encode`, `seal`).
  `AttestationDescriptor.firstFault` checks the mode, the seal, the segment's chain from the anchor, its range and
  its head (`DescriptorFault`); `Attestation.ofDescriptor` has a sink sign the seal and
  `Attestation.verifyDescriptor` refuses in `AttestationFault`. `Conformance.attestationLawsAt` gains two descriptor
  laws (`cases` 1000 → 1400) and a `segment-tampered` guard dimension. Nothing existing moved: the bare-head and
  party-bound attestation verbs, every snapshot and checkpoint seal, and every chain byte are unchanged.
