## 0.44.0 — DRAFT

**Slot class: breaking.** The minor after the released `0.43.0`. It advances the untagged `0.43.1` draft, a PATCH
slot of class internal, which a member of a higher class does not ride: through the `0.x` line a breaking class
advances the MINOR, which makes it a MINOR slot, and every later member rides it
([`STABILITY.md`](../../STABILITY.md), "Versioning policy"). No `v0.43.1` tag was cut, so the entries under
`0.43.1` ship in `0.44.0`, and its file keeps them as the record of their class. Phase 685 advances it. The
conformance `kitVersion` stamp lines move to `0.44.0` with the slot, and the corpus pin moves to the re-stamp of
the declared copies.

### Breaking

- **The query declaration's writer never writes a refused declaration as an admitted one** (Phase 685,
  `Fuaran.Core.Query`, DECISIONS.md D264) — class **retype** (managed) and **breaking** (wire). The writer turned a
  `Null` literal into the empty string, which a string column admits as `equalTo ""`, so a declaration the
  admission gate refuses was written as text a reader admits as another declaration; a budget that is not positive
  was admitted; and the effect reader's unknown-tag sentence doubled its lead (D263.1, D264.1).

  **What moved, by member** (`api/Fuaran.Core.Query.txt`):
  - `AdmittedQuery` is new: a declaration the gate admits, with one public member, `Declaration : Query`, and no
    public case. `QueryRegistry.admit : Query -> Result<AdmittedQuery, QueryError>` is the gate that makes one.
  - `QueryCodec.encode`, `Query.invocationKey`, `invocationKeyPage`, `invocationPreimage` and `grant` take an
    `AdmittedQuery` (they took a `Query`).
  - `QueryCodec.decode`, `decodeWith` and `decodeDetailedWith`, and `QueryRegistry.tryFind` and `enumerate`, answer
    an `AdmittedQuery` (they answered a `Query`).
  - `Query.TimeoutMs` and `PageSize` are `PositiveInt option` (they were `int option`). `PositiveInt` is new, with
    `Value` and `PositiveInt.tryCreate : int -> PositiveInt option`.
  - `QuerySeamCodec.encodeColumnPredicate` is no longer public. `decodeColumnPredicate`,
    `decodeColumnPredicateStrict` and every other seam entry are unchanged.
  - Not moved: `register`, `replace`, the dispatch, decision and capture verbs, `withGate`, `Query.invoke*`,
    `validateParams*`, `decodeArgs*`, and the conformance kit's public surface.

  **What a consumer must do, at each member:**
  - Writing a declaration: `QueryRegistry.admit q` first, then `QueryCodec.encode` on the `Ok`; the `Error` is the
    refusal `register` would have answered.
  - Keying a read or minting a grant: pass the admitted declaration to `invocationKey`, `invocationKeyPage`,
    `invocationPreimage` or `grant`; a registry's `tryFind` and `enumerate` already answer one.
  - Reading a declaration (`QueryCodec.decode*`, `tryFind`, `enumerate`): read its fields through `.Declaration`
    (`a.Declaration.Where`). Two admissions of one declaration are equal.
  - Building a budget: `PositiveInt.tryCreate n` in place of `Some n` (`None` for a value below 1); reading one:
    `.Value`.
  - A document carrying a `timeoutMs` or `pageSize` of `0` or below is refused by the reader, `OutOfRange` at that
    member ("out of range: 0; expected an int in [1, 2147483647]"), where it was admitted.
  - A predicate document: read with `decodeColumnPredicate`; there is no public writer of a bare predicate. A
    predicate travels inside a declaration (its `where`) or inside a refusal.

  **The wire (`api/wire/Fuaran.Core.Query.txt`, class `breaking`):** the sampler writes the `query` root through
  the gate. `query / Cell.Null` is removed: no admitted declaration carries a `Null` literal. 17 documents are
  re-stated as admitted declarations reaching the same discriminator (a literal on a column of its own type, a date,
  timestamp or decimal literal in canonical form): `query / Cell.Bool`, `Cell.Date`, `Cell.Decimal`, `Cell.Float`,
  `Cell.Str`, `Cell.Timestamp`, `ColumnPredicate.Contains`, `ColumnType.BoolType`, `DateType`, `DecimalType`,
  `FloatType`, `StringType`, `TimestampType`, and `TimeUnit.Microseconds`, `Milliseconds`, `Nanoseconds`, `Seconds`.
  `query / PositiveInt.PositiveInt` is added with the base document's bytes. `queryError / Cell.Null` stands. The
  documents whose embedded table the gate now refuses are re-stated by Phase 688, below.

  **Stated limits.** The query-error writer stays total for an error built by hand, and what such a document reads
  back as is not promised (D264.8 point 3, (B-iv)).

  **The Rust port** adopted `queryDeclarationLaws` at 84 vectors at its `282ff93`; Core's record of it is
  unchanged. Its own steps are D264.8 point 9.

- **The admission gate reads a declaration's embedded table** (Phase 688, `Fuaran.Core.Query`, DECISIONS.md D266) —
  class **union-widening** (managed) and **breaking** (wire). The writer took only an admitted declaration, but the
  gate did not read the source, so a declaration whose embedded table `Table.validate` refuses was admitted and
  written as text the reader refused, or read as another declaration: a column dropped, added or retyped, a decimal or
  an instant changed (D266.1, D266.2).

  **What moved, by member** (`api/Fuaran.Core.Query.txt`):
  - `QueryError.IllFormedSource of reason: string` is new, appended after `OrderNotHonoured`: the declaration's
    embedded table is one the column layer refuses, `reason` that layer's sentence (`ColumnCodec.errorString`). On
    the wire it is `illFormedSource`, with the one member `reason`.
  - `QueryRegistry.admit`, `register` and `replace` refuse such a declaration `IllFormedSource`, at `source`, before
    the parameters; a `Ref` is not read. No signature moves.

  **What a consumer must do:**
  - Match `IllFormedSource` wherever a `QueryError` is matched exhaustively, and read it wherever a query-error
    document is read: a reader of `0.43.0` refuses the tag as unknown.
  - A declaration whose embedded table `Table.validate` refuses, which `0.43.0` registered, is now refused at
    `source` by `admit`, `register` and `replace`: validate the table (`Table.validate`, or write it through
    `ColumnCodec.tryEncode`) before declaring it.
  - A declaration of that kind written under `0.43.0` may have been read by another host as another declaration: a
    host that stored one validates it.
  - An admitted declaration's text reads back as the declaration written, equal up to the column codec's normal form:
    an embedded table reads back with its columns in schema order. Compare a declaration read back with that form.

  **The wire (`api/wire/Fuaran.Core.Query.txt`):** `queryError / QueryError.IllFormedSource` is added (additive).
  Sixteen sampled `query` documents are re-stated with a table their schema entry carries (breaking):
  `ColumnData.Bools`, `Dates`, `Decimals`, `Floats`, `Strs` and `Timestamps`, whose entry takes the column's type;
  and `ColumnType.BoolType`, `DateType`, `DecimalType`, `FloatType`, `StringType` and `TimestampType` and
  `TimeUnit.Microseconds`, `Milliseconds`, `Nanoseconds` and `Seconds`, whose column is rebuilt at the entry's type.
  Ten were documents the reader refused (not six, as Phase 685's entry first counted), and six the reader read as
  another declaration (D266.10 point 4).

  **The families:** `seamRefusals` grows from 142 vectors to 143 (`illFormedSource`), and `queryDeclarationLaws` from
  97 to 107: its `admissionFault` and `register` inputs gain an optional `source`, a table as data, and ten vectors
  state eight tables the gate refuses at `source` and two it admits, in schema order and out of it. Every host's
  adoption record keeps its commit and its count; the Rust port reads `illFormedSource` as an unknown tag until it
  follows.

### Additive

- **The codec-only generator's per-root writer visibility** (Phase 685, `Fuaran.Core.Idl.Codegen`) — class
  **additive**: `Gen.fsharpCodecModuleWith sup request internalWriters moduleName idl` emits `encode<Name>`
  `internal` for each root `internalWriters` names, its readers public; an empty list emits exactly what

### Internal

- **The dispatch holds a resolver's unsupported predicate to the declaration's own** (Phase 685, D264.8 point 3,
  (B-i)): `Query.invokePageWithArgs`, which every typed dispatch reaches, answers `PredicateUnsupported p` as
  `PredicateNotHonoured p` only when `p` is in the declaration's `Where`, and otherwise as `ExecutionFailed` naming
  its tag and column. So every `PredicateNotHonoured` a dispatch raises names an admitted declaration's predicate.
  `queryLaws`' shape law gains the case. A resolver that answered with a predicate of its own now earns
  `ExecutionFailed`.
- **The effect reader's unknown-tag sentences name what was found once** (Phase 685, D264.4,
  `Fuaran.Core.Function`): `unknown host effect 'everywhere'; expected one of 'pure', 'readsHost', 'writesHost'` and
  `unknown determinism 'sometimes'; expected a determinism tag`. Codes and paths do not move.
- **`queryDeclarationLaws` grows from 84 vectors to 97** (Phase 685, D264.8 point 7): a budget of `0` and of `-1`
  refused at `timeoutMs` and at `pageSize`, and both at `1` read; and the six refusals the Rust port's adoption found
  unpinned, as eight vectors: `params` absent, `resultSchema` not a list, `effect` absent, the reason a negative
  infinity is refused with, a date not in canonical form (the gate's clause and the reader's `OutOfRange` at
  `where.0.value`) and decimal text with a trailing zero (the gate's clause, and the reader's acceptance,
  canonicalised). The three admission vectors are reference-only, as their siblings are. No column family and no
  `seamRefusals` vector moves; the family is not a declared copy.
- **The query model reads a declaration's source** (Phase 688, D266.10 point 6, `proofs/Query.fst`): a source is
  a `Ref`, or an embedded table carried with the column layer's verdict; the admission refuses a refused one first,
  so an admitted declaration's source is accepted; `column_codec_laws` states a source's round trip as the column
  strand proves it, to its normal form, and `query_roundtrip_identity` concludes the declaration with its source
  normalised. The seam and codec differentials draw embedded tables, valid and not. Rows restated in `proofs.json`.
- **The proof model** (`proofs/Query.fst`): the budgets are `option pos`, read by `d_pos`; `admitted_query` is the
  admitted declaration, and `admitted_carried` discharges `all_carried` from admission, so
  `query_roundtrip_identity` spends `column_codec_laws` alone; the identity theorems
  (`invocation_key_injective`, `invocation_key_page_injective`, `invocation_identity_injective`,
  `grant_for_another_invocation_refused`) are stated over admitted declarations, the filter renderer's premise
  weakened to carried filters (`injective_on_carried`); `unsupported_predicate` models the dispatch's hold.
- **The Rust port's adoption is recorded for the admitted query declaration** (Phase 691; the conformance manifests

  **Nothing to migrate.**
- **The dispatch holds a resolver's rows to the table's validation** (Phase 692, `Fuaran.Core.Query`, DECISIONS.md
  D267) — class **internal**: no `api/` or `api/wire/` row moves (the baseline tool reports both unchanged), and no
  vector family. A resolver answering rows `Table.validate` refuses is now refused, `ExecutionFailed` naming the
  column layer's reason ("the resolver answered rows the column layer refuses: ragged table: column 'b' has 1 rows
  where the first column has 2", with no `recoverable` arguments), by every dispatch verb: `Query.invoke`,
  `invokePage`, `invokeWithArgs` and `invokePageWithArgs`, and every `QueryRegistry.dispatch*` verb, paged or not.
  Until now the seam answered such rows `Ok (Ready _)`, and the result's writer wrote them as text that reads back as
  other rows, or that its reader refuses. Rows it accepts are answered as the resolver gave them, in any column
  order.

  **What a consumer must do:** a resolver answers rows `Table.validate` accepts (validate them before
  answering); a host matching `ExecutionFailed` meets one more cause. The captured verbs journal such rows
  as the refusal, never as a completion, so a replay answers the refusal answered live. A journal written before
  `0.44.0` may hold such rows as a completion, which replays as the rows its text reads back as: re-capture it if that
  matters. `QueryCodec.encodeResult` and `encodeDeferredResult` stay total: a page a dispatch answered reads back as
  itself with its rows in schema order; a page built by hand, or one a host settles a pending capture with, whose
  rows `Table.validate` refuses may read back as other rows or be refused, and rows out of schema order read back in
  schema order. The proof model's `invoke` takes the column layer's verdict on a page's rows, and the column codec's
  premise states a page's rows as the column strand proves them, to their normal form (`proofs.json`:
  `query-dispatch-holds-rows` is new; five rows restated).
